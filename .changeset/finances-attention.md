Finances shows what needs attention. A notifications bell in every Finances page header counts and lists, by Wallet, the uploaded lines To review, Overdue items, forecast Wallets with no Balance check, and low balances within the horizon. Each item opens where it is fixed: To review opens the Wallet's Activity, an Overdue Occurrence its Schedule page with that Occurrence's editor open, an Overdue one-off the Wallet's Upcoming one-offs, a missing Balance check the Balance check dialog, and a low balance the Wallet's home tab at that day. Each Wallet's home tab has a banner with its To review and Overdue counts, and the All tab's cards show the To review count beside Overdue.
