Wallets take Balance checks. From a Wallet's Balance checks tab, enter the balance the bank shows on a date; on save you see the computed balance and how far off the bank's figure is, and nothing is adjusted automatically. A second check on the same date replaces the first. The list shows each check by date with "off by" when it differs, and the Wallets list and Wallet page now show the balance from the latest check. A Wallet with Balance checks can no longer be deleted; archive it instead.
