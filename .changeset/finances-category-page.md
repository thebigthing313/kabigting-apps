Finances: tapping a Category on the Monthly summary opens its page, with a year of monthly bars
against a dashed average line, the month's figures and its Actuals, each opening the Activity
editor. A Category can be renamed, archived or unarchived there, or merged into another: a confirm
names how many Transactions and Schedules move, then they move and the Category is archived as
merged. Uncategorised has a page too, without actions. The Categories list links to each page.
