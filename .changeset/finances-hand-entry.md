Enter Actuals and one-off Planned Transactions by hand. A + button on Finances home and on each Wallet page offers Add Actual, Add one-off Planned and Enter Balance check, each starting on that Wallet. The form takes the Wallet, date, Amount with money in or out (worded as owed for a Wallet shown as owed), Account, Category and note. A Wallet's Activity tab lists its upcoming one-offs, then its Actuals newest first a page at a time; tapping a row opens an editor that changes any field or deletes it. Balances, Balance check comparisons and the Forecast now count Actuals, and one-off Planned items land in the Forecast on their date. Overdue ones count today unless they are dated on or before the latest Balance check.
