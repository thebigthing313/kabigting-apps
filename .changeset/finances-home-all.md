Finances home now starts with an All tab: one card per Wallet. A forecast Wallet's card shows today's balance, the low point and its date, a warning for the first day under $0 or under the threshold, its next three items and how many are Overdue; tapping it opens that Wallet's tab. A Wallet outside the forecast shows a greyed "Not in the forecast" card with its latest Balance check. The cards stack on a phone. Each tab's menu has "Open here by default", which sets where Finances home opens for everyone in the household; the current default shows a pin. Home opens on All until someone picks a Wallet.
