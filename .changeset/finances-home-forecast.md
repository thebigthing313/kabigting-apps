Finances home now shows a Forecast for each Wallet in the forecast, one tab per Wallet. A Wallet's Forecast starts from its latest Balance check and shows today's balance, the low point and its date, a 30, 90, 180 or 365-day horizon (90 by default), a balance chart with the threshold and $0 lines, warnings for the first day under the threshold and the first day under $0, and a day-by-day list of end-of-day balances with each day's Schedule Occurrences. Overdue Occurrences dated after the latest Balance check count today and are marked Overdue, overridden and skipped Occurrences show as soon as they are saved, and a Transfer Schedule moves money out of one Wallet and into the other. A forecast Wallet with no Balance check shows a dashed "Enter balance" tab that opens the Balance check dialog.
