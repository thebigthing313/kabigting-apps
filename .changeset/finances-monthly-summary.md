The Finances Summary section now shows one month at a time, stepped with arrows: it opens on last month, goes back to the first month with Actuals and forward to this month, which reads "so far". A totals table compares Income, Spending and Net with last month and the 12-month average. Below it, income Categories are listed with their difference from average, and spending Categories get a bar with a tick at their average, highlighted when far over it, then Uncategorised. Only Actuals count, in every Wallet; Transfers and Planned Transactions don't. A Category counts as income in a month it nets positive, so refunds net inside their Category. Archived Categories with Actuals are marked, and a Manage Categories link opens the Categories list.
