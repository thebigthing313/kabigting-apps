Each Schedule now has its own page, opened from the Schedules list. It lists the Schedule's Occurrences from the last 3 months to the next 6, with Overridden, Skipped and Overdue badges. Tap an Occurrence to give it a different amount or date, to Skip or Unskip it, or to put it "Back to schedule". Only that Occurrence changes; the rest of the Schedule keeps its terms, and a moved Occurrence still appears once, on its new date. On a card payment (a Transfer into a Wallet shown as owed), the fields read Statement balance and Due date. Amounts are entered as the Wallet shows them, and the Schedules list's next date and amount include these changes.
