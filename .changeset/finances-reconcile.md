Reconcile Actuals to what was Planned. Adding an Actual lists the Planned items in the same Wallet with the same sign within a week of its date, closest amount first, and ticking one Reconciles it on save. From a Wallet's Activity, any Actual can be Reconciled to any outstanding Planned item, or Un-Reconciled, which puts the Planned item back at its own amount. A Reconciled Actual keeps its amount and date and takes the Planned item's Category when it has none. Reconciled items leave the Forecast, Activity shows what each Actual is Reconciled to, and a Reconciled Occurrence shows the Actual's amount on its Schedule. A Schedule can no longer be ended or changed from a date before one of its Reconciled Occurrences.
