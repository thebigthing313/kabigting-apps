A Schedule's page can now Change from a date, End, Reopen and Delete it, and shows its History. Change from a date ends the current terms the day before and starts new ones (amount, how often, first date, end date); earlier Occurrences keep the old terms, and overrides from that date on carry over. End lists the overrides and skips after the last day that it will delete; Reconciled Occurrences are never touched. Reopen clears the end date. History lists each set of terms, newest first, with its dates and amount. Delete removes the Schedule with its whole History, and is offered only when nothing is Reconciled to it. In the Schedules list, a Schedule changed from a date stays one row, and Schedules whose end date has passed sit in a dimmed Ended group.
