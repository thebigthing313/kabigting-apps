Finances now has Schedules. Add one from the Schedules list: the Wallet it draws on, a Transaction (money in or out) or a Transfer to another Wallet, the Account, the Amount, every so many weeks or months from a start date, an optional end date, a Category (not for a Transfer) and a note. The list groups Schedules by Wallet and shows each one's Account or "Transfer to <Wallet>", its terms in words (such as "Monthly on the 31st" or "Every 2 weeks on Friday") and its next date and amount. A monthly Schedule on the 29th to 31st falls on the last day of shorter months, and dates never move for weekends.
