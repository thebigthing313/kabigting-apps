Uploaded lines with a suggestion now wait in a To review panel at the top of the Wallet's Activity. A line near a Planned item offers it, with Confirm to Reconcile, Pick another to choose any outstanding Planned item in the Wallet, and Unplanned to clear it. A line that repeats an Actual you entered by hand reads "Same as your hand entry?"; confirming keeps the uploaded line with your Account, Category, note and Reconcile, and removes the hand entry. Lines with no suggestion are unplanned straight away, and the upload result says how many wait for review.
