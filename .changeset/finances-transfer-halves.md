A card payment that shows up in both the checking file and the card file now stays one Transfer. An Actual's editor has Mark as Transfer, which turns it into a Transfer to or from another Wallet. Once one side of a Transfer has arrived from an upload, the other Wallet's matching line waits in To review as "Other side of Transfer from …?", and Confirm joins it to the Transfer. Until then the Transfer is flagged "Only one side arrived" in Activity, and uploading either file again adds nothing.
