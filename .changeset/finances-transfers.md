Enter Transfers between two Wallets by hand. In the Add form, choose "Transfer to…" to enter an Actual Transfer or a one-off Planned Transfer from one Wallet to another. A Transfer has no Category, so it never counts as spending or income. It shows once in each Wallet's Activity, as "Transfer to" or "Transfer from" the other Wallet, and its editor changes or deletes it. Both Wallets' balances count an Actual Transfer. A Planned Transfer lands in both Wallets' Forecasts, and once Overdue it counts today, unless it is dated on or before that Wallet's latest Balance check.
