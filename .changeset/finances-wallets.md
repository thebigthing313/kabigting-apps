Finances now has Wallets. Add a Wallet from the Wallets list, open it, and change its Settings: its name, whether it is in the forecast, its low-balance threshold, whether it is shown as owed (amounts are typed and shown as what you owe, as for a card), and archiving it. Archived Wallets hide behind a "Show archived" toggle. The Activity and Balance checks tabs are placeholders for now.
