Add Checklists to Notes. New → Checklist opens an editor with an optional title and an "Add item" row; each Item is one line, edited in place, and an Item left blank is dropped. Ticking an Item saves at once and folds it, struck through, under a collapsible "N checked" section, the most recently checked first; unticking returns it to the bottom of the unchecked Items. Each Item has a delete button, always shown on a phone and on hover on a desktop. Any change to an Item counts as an edit, so the Checklist rises to the top of the list, where its row shows "N to do · N done" and what is left to do. A new Checklist left with only blank Items is not kept.
