Add Clear checked to Checklists. Beside the "N checked" section, Clear checked deletes the checked Items in one step and counts as an edit, so the Checklist rises to the top of the list. Only the Items you saw checked are deleted, and only if they are still checked: an Item someone unticked first stays, back among the unchecked Items. If the clear fails, the Items come back and a message says so.
