An open Note now takes in other people's edits when you return to the app or reconnect: its title, its body, and a Checklist's Items as others edit, tick, add, delete, reorder or clear them. They arrive quietly, with no banner. Text you are typing, still saving or marked "Not saved" is never replaced; it saves over theirs, and the last save wins. The field you are in keeps still until you leave it, then shows the latest. An Item someone deleted while you were typing in it stays until your text tries to save, then goes with a note saying someone else deleted it, and never comes back; the same goes for a Note deleted or unshared while you were typing. A save the server refuses puts the field back as the server holds it; a refused add, tick, move, delete or clear is undone with a toast.
