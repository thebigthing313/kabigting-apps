An open Note now takes in other people's edits when you return to the app or reconnect: its title, its body, and a Checklist's Items as others edit, tick, add, delete, reorder or clear them. They arrive quietly, with no banner. Text you are typing, still saving or marked "Not saved" is never replaced; it saves over theirs, and the last save wins. An Item someone deleted while you were typing in it stays until your text tries to save, then goes with a toast, and never comes back. Typing left in a Note that was deleted or unshared while open is reported as not saved, rather than lost without a word. Deleting an Item now comes back with a toast if the server refuses it.
