Leave a shared Note. Someone a Note is shared with now has a ⋯ menu with Leave note: the Note leaves their list at once and they go back to Notes, while it stays as it was for its creator and everyone else. Only the creator can add them back. Leaving doesn't change when the Note was last edited, and it works even if the creator has lost access to Notes. If the server refuses, the Note comes back with a message. The creator's ⋯ menu still offers Delete note, not Leave.
