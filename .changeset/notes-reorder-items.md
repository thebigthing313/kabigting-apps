Reorder a Checklist's unchecked Items by dragging the grip handle beside each one, with a mouse, a finger on a phone, or the keyboard (space to pick up, the arrow keys to move, space to drop). A move saves at once and changes only the moved Item's place, so two people moving different Items both keep their moves; a move that fails is undone. Checked Items have no handle: they stay in the order they were checked.
