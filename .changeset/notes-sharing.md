Share a Note with an Audience. The Note's creator taps Share in the editor and picks, from a bottom sheet, any of the other Accounts holding Notes access; everyone picked can read and edit the Note, and only the creator can change who sees it or delete it. Once shared, the button shows their avatars and "Shared". Everyone else sees the creator's avatar and "Shared by …" instead, with no Share or Delete. Each row in the Notes list now says "Only you", "Shared with …" or "Shared by …", and the list holds every Note you can see, yours and those shared with you. Someone taken off a Note stops seeing it the next time Notes refreshes. Changing who sees a Note doesn't change when it was last edited. Nobody outside a Note, the Admin included, can open it.
