Mark Notes shared with you as New. When someone adds you to a Note, it shows a "New" marker in your Notes list and sits above all your other Notes until you open it. Opening it clears the marker for you alone. If you are taken off a Note and later added back, it shows as New again; losing and regaining access to Notes doesn't. Your own Notes are never marked New, and later edits don't bring the marker back.
