Finances: "Show older Actuals" on a Wallet's Activity tab and "Show older checks" on its Balance checks tab now add the next older page under the ones shown, newest first, without the list going blank. Before, a Wallet with a long history could jump from its recent Actuals straight to ones years old. Actuals on the same day keep one order from page to page, and a load the server refuses now shows an error instead of an empty list.
