Split: an open Expense or Payment keeps your draft when someone else saves a change, says who changed it and when, and offers Load their version. One deleted while you edit it closes with a note that your changes weren't saved, deleting one that's already gone just counts as deleted, and losing access to Split takes you to Home. A save that fails offline stays put with everything kept and a Try again button, and a retry that had already reached the server counts as saved.
