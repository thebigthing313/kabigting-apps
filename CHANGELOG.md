# kabigting-apps

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- de44c70: The Admin now holds every App through the role. On the Accounts screen the Admin's row shows "All Apps" and its Edit Apps sheet has no App checkboxes, and setting Apps on the Admin is refused. App access rows the Admin had before are removed.
- ac73097: Inside an App, the sidebar now lists that App's sections under an App switcher for moving between Home and your Apps. Finances has Home, Schedules, Wallets and Summary sections, each a placeholder for now. Choosing a sidebar item on a phone closes the drawer, and an Account with no Apps sees "No Apps yet".
- 43f5505: Add Finances Accounts and Categories. A new Categories list (`/finances/categories`) shows every Category, marks archived ones, and offers Add and Unarchive. Account and Category pickers add a typed name inline and rename the picked entry; archived Categories stay out of the picker. Names are unique. The pickers appear in forms from later Finances releases.
- 02e1a78: Finances shows what needs attention. A notifications bell in every Finances page header counts and lists, by Wallet, the uploaded lines To review, Overdue items, forecast Wallets with no Balance check, and low balances within the horizon. Each item opens where it is fixed: To review opens the Wallet's Activity, an Overdue Occurrence its Schedule page with that Occurrence's editor open, an Overdue one-off the Wallet's Upcoming one-offs, a missing Balance check the Balance check dialog, and a low balance the Wallet's home tab at that day. Each Wallet's home tab has a banner with its To review and Overdue counts, and the All tab's cards show the To review count beside Overdue.
- ec1a95f: Wallets take Balance checks. From a Wallet's Balance checks tab, enter the balance the bank shows on a date; on save you see the computed balance and how far off the bank's figure is, and nothing is adjusted automatically. A second check on the same date replaces the first. The list shows each check by date with "off by" when it differs, and the Wallets list and Wallet page now show the balance from the latest check. A Wallet with Balance checks can no longer be deleted; archive it instead.
- 9ec9a33: Finances: tapping a Category on the Monthly summary opens its page, with a year of monthly bars
  against a dashed average line, the month's figures and its Actuals, each opening the Activity
  editor. A Category can be renamed, archived or unarchived there, or merged into another: a confirm
  names how many Transactions and Schedules move, then they move and the Category is archived as
  merged. Uncategorised has a page too, without actions. The Categories list links to each page.
- 2b8de1c: Enter Actuals and one-off Planned Transactions by hand. A + button on Finances home and on each Wallet page offers Add Actual, Add one-off Planned and Enter Balance check, each starting on that Wallet. The form takes the Wallet, date, Amount with money in or out (worded as owed for a Wallet shown as owed), Account, Category and note. A Wallet's Activity tab lists its upcoming one-offs, then its Actuals newest first a page at a time; tapping a row opens an editor that changes any field or deletes it. Balances, Balance check comparisons and the Forecast now count Actuals, and one-off Planned items land in the Forecast on their date. Overdue ones count today unless they are dated on or before the latest Balance check.
- 6691740: Finances: a one-time import brings the household's old history over. `scripts/finances-extract.ps1`
  reads the old Access file read-only, through a local map of its table and column names, into
  neutral JSON in `exports/`. `pnpm finances:import` then loads every past Actual onto Checking,
  already reviewed so none lands in To review, along with the 18 agreed Categories (including the new
  Card payments), the Accounts history uses and each day's last Balance check. It has a dry run,
  refuses when Finances holds data unless given `--wipe`, writes in one transaction, and prints a
  counts-only report that fails on any mismatch.
- bcdbfc0: Finances home now starts with an All tab: one card per Wallet. A forecast Wallet's card shows today's balance, the low point and its date, a warning for the first day under $0 or under the threshold, its next three items and how many are Overdue; tapping it opens that Wallet's tab. A Wallet outside the forecast shows a greyed "Not in the forecast" card with its latest Balance check. The cards stack on a phone. Each tab's menu has "Open here by default", which sets where Finances home opens for everyone in the household; the current default shows a pin. Home opens on All until someone picks a Wallet.
- 45fa84b: Finances home now shows a Forecast for each Wallet in the forecast, one tab per Wallet. A Wallet's Forecast starts from its latest Balance check and shows today's balance, the low point and its date, a 30, 90, 180 or 365-day horizon (90 by default), a balance chart with the threshold and $0 lines, warnings for the first day under the threshold and the first day under $0, and a day-by-day list of end-of-day balances with each day's Schedule Occurrences. Overdue Occurrences dated after the latest Balance check count today and are marked Overdue, overridden and skipped Occurrences show as soon as they are saved, and a Transfer Schedule moves money out of one Wallet and into the other. A forecast Wallet with no Balance check shows a dashed "Enter balance" tab that opens the Balance check dialog.
- 74bd244: The Finances Summary section now shows one month at a time, stepped with arrows: it opens on last month, goes back to the first month with Actuals and forward to this month, which reads "so far". A totals table compares Income, Spending and Net with last month and the 12-month average. Below it, income Categories are listed with their difference from average, and spending Categories get a bar with a tick at their average, highlighted when far over it, then Uncategorised. Only Actuals count, in every Wallet; Transfers and Planned Transactions don't. A Category counts as income in a month it nets positive, so refunds net inside their Category. Archived Categories with Actuals are marked, and a Manage Categories link opens the Categories list.
- 85ad0a5: Each Schedule now has its own page, opened from the Schedules list. It lists the Schedule's Occurrences from the last 3 months to the next 6, with Overridden, Skipped and Overdue badges. Tap an Occurrence to give it a different amount or date, to Skip or Unskip it, or to put it "Back to schedule". Only that Occurrence changes; the rest of the Schedule keeps its terms, and a moved Occurrence still appears once, on its new date. On a card payment (a Transfer into a Wallet shown as owed), the fields read Statement balance and Due date. Amounts are entered as the Wallet shows them, and the Schedules list's next date and amount include these changes.
- c5efddb: Reconcile Actuals to what was Planned. Adding an Actual lists the Planned items in the same Wallet with the same sign within a week of its date, closest amount first, and ticking one Reconciles it on save. From a Wallet's Activity, any Actual can be Reconciled to any outstanding Planned item, or Un-Reconciled, which puts the Planned item back at its own amount. A Reconciled Actual keeps its amount and date and takes the Planned item's Category when it has none. Reconciled items leave the Forecast, Activity shows what each Actual is Reconciled to, and a Reconciled Occurrence shows the Actual's amount on its Schedule. Planned Transfers, one-off or from a Transfer Schedule, can be Reconciled too: an Actual Transfer to one between the same Wallets, or a line on either Wallet, which then becomes the Actual Transfer. A Schedule can no longer be ended or changed from a date before one of its Reconciled Occurrences.
- 7c315c2: Replace the Notes sample App with an empty Finances App. Accounts with Finances access see a Finances home that says "Nothing here yet"; Notes and its data are removed.
- 478d963: A Schedule's page can now Change from a date, End, Reopen and Delete it, and shows its History. Change from a date ends the current terms the day before and starts new ones (amount, how often, first date, end date); earlier Occurrences keep the old terms, and overrides from that date on carry over. When the new terms fall on different days, the change lists the overrides that no longer fit, and saving deletes them. End lists the overrides and skips after the last day that it will delete; Reconciled Occurrences are never touched. Reopen clears the end date. History lists each set of terms, newest first, with its dates and amount. Delete removes the Schedule with its whole History, and is offered only when nothing is Reconciled to it. In the Schedules list, a Schedule changed from a date stays one row, and Schedules whose end date has passed sit in a dimmed Ended group.
- 17c94c8: Finances now has Schedules. Add one from the Schedules list: the Wallet it draws on, a Transaction (money in or out) or a Transfer to another Wallet, the Account, the Amount, every so many weeks or months from a start date, an optional end date, a Category (not for a Transfer) and a note. On a Wallet shown as owed, such as a card, the amount is entered as the Wallet shows it: a charge "Adds to what's owed". The list groups Schedules by Wallet and shows each one's Account or "Transfer to <Wallet>", its terms in words (such as "Monthly on the 31st" or "Every 2 weeks on Friday") and its next date and amount. A monthly Schedule on the 29th to 31st falls on the last day of shorter months, and dates never move for weekends.
- 43340e7: Uploaded lines with a suggestion now wait in a To review panel at the top of the Wallet's Activity. A line near a Planned item offers it, with Confirm to Reconcile, Pick another to choose any outstanding Planned item in the Wallet, and Unplanned to clear it. A line that repeats an Actual you entered by hand reads "Same as your hand entry?"; confirming keeps the uploaded line with your Account, Category, note and Reconcile, and removes the hand entry. Lines with no suggestion are unplanned straight away, and the upload result says how many wait for review.
- a04bbcb: A card payment that shows up in both the checking file and the card file now stays one Transfer. An Actual's editor has Mark as Transfer, which turns it into a Transfer to or from another Wallet. Once one side of a Transfer has arrived from an upload, the other Wallet's matching line waits in To review as "Other side of Transfer from …?", and Confirm joins it to the Transfer. Until then the Transfer is flagged "Only one side arrived" in Activity, and uploading either file again adds nothing.
- b7a0a20: Enter Transfers between two Wallets by hand. In the Add form, choose "Transfer to…" to enter an Actual Transfer or a one-off Planned Transfer from one Wallet to another. A Transfer has no Category, so it never counts as spending or income. It shows once in each Wallet's Activity, as "Transfer to" or "Transfer from" the other Wallet, and its editor changes or deletes it. Both Wallets' balances count an Actual Transfer. A Planned Transfer lands in both Wallets' Forecasts, and once Overdue it counts today, unless it is dated on or before that Wallet's latest Balance check.
- fbd09dc: Upload bank and card statement files. The + button's new Upload file takes an `.ofx`, `.qfx` or `.qbo` download of up to 5 MB. The first file from a bank account asks which Wallet it belongs to (shown by its last four digits), and the Wallet remembers it, so later files go straight there. Its lines become Actuals, with amounts signed as the bank gives them, and the result reads "N added, M already here": uploading the same file again adds nothing. An uploaded Actual keeps the bank's amount, date and Wallet, but its Category, Account and note can change. Deleting one ignores it, so later uploads skip it, and the Wallet's Activity has an Ignored lines list where it can be restored. Only a hash and the last four digits of a bank account are stored.
- 7e225f4: Finances now has Wallets. Add a Wallet from the Wallets list, open it, and change its Settings: its name, whether it is in the forecast, its low-balance threshold, whether it is shown as owed (amounts are typed and shown as what you owe, as for a card), and archiving it. Archived Wallets hide behind a "Show archived" toggle. The Activity and Balance checks tabs are placeholders for now.

### Patch Changes

- f159fab: A bank line for a Transfer you entered by hand no longer counts the money twice. When you upload a file whose line matches a hand-entered Transfer (or an Actual you marked as a Transfer by hand) in that Wallet, for the exact amount, moving the Wallet the Transfer's way, within 3 days, it waits in To review as "Same as your hand-entered Transfer?". Confirm joins the line to that side of the Transfer, and uploading the file again adds nothing.
- 9be4ace: The Finances import maps the old "Freelance Income" and "Sales Income" categories to Reimbursements.
- 366393d: The one-time Finances import now accepts hand-typed Balance check amounts past the cent, rounding them to the nearest cent (halves away from zero) and counting them in the report. Transaction and ledger amounts must still be whole cents.
- e55691a: Saving a Finances form straight after adding an Account or Category inline no longer fails: the save waits until the new name is stored. If the new name is refused, nothing is saved, the picker is cleared and the form stays open with what was typed.
