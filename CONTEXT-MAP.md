# Context map

Each context keeps its own glossary. A term means what its own context's glossary says, even when
another context uses the same word for something else.

- **Shell** — [`CONTEXT.md`](CONTEXT.md): sign-in, Accounts as identities, Apps and App access.
- **Finances** — [`docs/finances/CONTEXT.md`](docs/finances/CONTEXT.md): the household finances App.
  Here **Account** means a payee or income source, not a sign-in.
