# Baby

The Baby App: a shared log of one baby's Feedings and Diaper changes, kept by both parents and the
nursery tablet. There is exactly one baby; it has no record of its own and is never named.

## Language

**Feeding**:
One time the baby ate, timed by when it started. Its method is Breast or Bottle.
_Avoid_: Feed, nursing, meal

**Breast**:
A Feeding method: the baby nursed directly, on Sides given in order.
_Avoid_: Nursing, latch

**Sides**:
Which breast or breasts a Breast Feeding used, in order: Left, Right, Left then Right, or Right
then Left.
_Avoid_: Both (it loses which side came last)

**Bottle**:
A Feeding method: the baby drank an amount from a bottle holding either Formula or Breast milk.
A top-up after a Breast Feeding is its own Bottle Feeding.
_Avoid_: Formula feed (Formula is a bottle content, not a method)

**Diaper change**:
One moment a diaper was changed, marked with Pee, Poop, both or neither. A change with neither is
Dry.
_Avoid_: Diaper, nappy, change

**Pee** / **Poop**:
The two things a Diaper change can record finding.
_Avoid_: Wet/dirty, #1/#2

**Dry**:
A Diaper change with neither Pee nor Poop.
_Avoid_: Empty, clean

**History**:
Every Feeding and Diaper change in one list, newest first, grouped by household day, where entries
are reviewed, corrected and deleted.
_Avoid_: Log, timeline, feed
